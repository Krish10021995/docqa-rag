# Acme Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Day one

Start with the IT onboarding session at 09:00. You will receive your laptop,
badge, and access to the internal wiki. Set up your two-factor authentication
and join the company Slack and GitHub organization.

## 2. First week checklist

Complete the security awareness training, join your team's standup, and book a
30 minute 1:1 with your manager. All new employees are assigned a buddy from
their team who can answer day-to-day questions.

## 3. Tools and accounts

Employees are provisioned accounts for email, Slack, GitHub, the internal wiki,
the HR portal, and the expense system within the first 48 hours. Access to
production systems is granted only after the security training is completed and
the access request is approved by the team lead.

## 4. Probation review

The probation period is three months. At the two-month mark, your manager will
schedule a probation review to discuss progress, set goals for the final month,
and confirm the compensation package.

## 5. Training allowance

Every employee receives an annual training budget of 1500 euros for courses,
certifications, and conferences. Approval is needed for courses over 500 euros.
