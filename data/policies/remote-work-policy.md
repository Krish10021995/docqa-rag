# Acme Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility

Remote work is available to all full-time employees whose role can be performed
outside the office. Engineering, data, product, design, marketing, and finance
roles are eligible. IT and on-site security roles are not remote eligible by
default.

## 2. Work location

Employees may work from home anywhere in the country, or from an Acme satellite
office. To work from abroad for more than 30 consecutive days, approval from
the People team and the manager is required at least 45 days in advance.

## 3. Core hours and expectations

All remote employees must be available during core collaboration hours: 10:00
to 15:00 in their local time zone. Meetings must be attended by video. A stable
internet connection of at least 20 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload is expected.

## 4. Equipment and support

Acme provides a company laptop, a monitor, a headset, and a one-time home office
stipend of 500 euros. IT support is available for the company equipment;
personal equipment is not supported. The stipend can be used for desks, chairs,
and other ergonomic equipment, and it is taxable according to local law.

## 5. Remote terminates on notice

Remote arrangements can be ended by either side with one month's notice. The
employee returns to the closest office. Expenses for the return move are
covered by Acme up to 1000 euros.
